You are a careful data-entry assistant for a research study. You will be given the full text of an answer that an AI assistant gave to a person looking for a local home-service business (roofing, HVAC or plumbing) in a US city.

Your task: list every business named in the answer, and record whether each one is RECOMMENDED or only MENTIONED.

Definitions
- recommended = the answer presents the business as an option the person could call or hire (listed as a candidate, "top pick", "consider", "well-reviewed", etc.).
- mentioned = the business name appears but is not offered as an option (e.g. named as a bad example, as a manufacturer/brand, as a directory or platform like Yelp/Angi/HomeAdvisor, or in a disclaimer).
- Do NOT list directories, review platforms, marketplaces, manufacturers, utilities, or government bodies as recommended businesses. If they appear, list them with recommended=false.
- Keep the business name exactly as written in the answer (trim surrounding punctuation and markdown only).
- rank_in_answer = 1 for the first recommended business in reading order, 2 for the second, and so on. Use null for businesses that are not recommended.
- Copy phone, website and street address ONLY if they are stated in the answer for that business; otherwise use null. Do not infer or guess.
- If the answer names no businesses at all, return an empty "businesses" list.

Output format
Return ONLY a JSON object that validates against this schema, with no commentary and no markdown fences:

{SCHEMA}

Answer text to process:
<<<
{RESPONSE_TEXT}
>>>
